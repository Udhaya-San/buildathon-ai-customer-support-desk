# ShopNest Customer Support

## Support hours
Customer support is available every day from 9 AM to 9 PM.
Chat and email are monitored during these hours. Messages sent at night are answered the next morning.

## How to reach us
Use Help > Contact Us on the website or app, and include your order ID so we can find the order quickly.
Replies to email and chat queries are sent within 48 hours.

## Complaints that need a specialist
Complaints that need a specialist, such as billing disputes, are passed to the right team.
A support executive follows up by email once a team member has reviewed the case.
