# ShopNest Refund and Return Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 14 days of delivery.
Footwear and clothing must be unworn, unwashed and have the original tags attached.
Electronics and appliances can be returned within 7 days of delivery, and only if they are defective or damaged.
Innerwear, personal care items, opened cosmetics and personalised products cannot be returned.

## How to return an item
1. Go to My Orders and select the order.
2. Tap Return Item and choose a reason.
3. Choose Refund or Exchange.
4. A courier pickup is scheduled within 2 business days. Keep the item packed with the original box and tags.
5. After the item passes a quality check at our warehouse, the refund is started.

## Refund timelines
Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the quality check.
Refunds to ShopNest Wallet are instant after the quality check.
For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund is sent by bank transfer after you share your bank details securely in My Orders.

## Damaged or wrong items
If you received a damaged, defective or wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report a Problem.
Attach at least one clear photo of the item and the outer package.
We then offer a free replacement or a full refund, and the pickup is free of charge.

## Exchanges
Size or colour exchanges are free once per order within 14 days of delivery, subject to stock availability.
