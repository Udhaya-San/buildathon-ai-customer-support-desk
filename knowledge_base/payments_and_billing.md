# ShopNest Payments and Billing

## Payment methods
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and ShopNest Wallet.
Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to Rs 10,000.

## Charged more than once
When a payment is attempted, your bank may show a temporary authorisation hold. A hold that is not converted into a charge is released by the bank in 3 to 5 business days.
If you were charged more than once for the same order, share your order ID with support. A confirmed duplicate charge is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
Payment disputes involving repeated charges, bank chargebacks or unrecognised transactions are reviewed by our billing team.

## Payment failed but money deducted
If the payment failed but the amount was deducted, the amount is automatically refunded within 5 to 7 business days.
No action is needed unless the amount has not returned after 7 business days.

## Invoices
A GST invoice is emailed after the order is dispatched. It can also be downloaded from My Orders > Download Invoice.

## Coupons
Only one coupon can be used per order, and coupons cannot be combined with other offers.
