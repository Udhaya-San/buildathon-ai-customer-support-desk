# ShopNest Product FAQ

## Warranty
Electronics and home appliances such as blenders, mixers and kettles carry a 12 month manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery.
The ShopNest invoice is your proof of purchase for warranty claims.
Warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, water damage or unauthorised repair.

## Defective on arrival
If an electronic item or appliance is defective or broken when it arrives, report it within 7 days of delivery from My Orders > Report a Problem with a photo or short video.
We arrange a free pickup and send a replacement of the same product within 3 to 5 business days after the pickup.
If the same product is not in stock, we refund the full amount instead.

## Genuine products
All products are sold by authorised brand sellers and every order comes with a GST invoice.

## Size and fit
Each footwear and clothing page has a size chart. If the fit is wrong, you can exchange the size once for free within 14 days of delivery.

## Product availability
If an item is out of stock, use Notify Me on the product page and we email you when it is back.
