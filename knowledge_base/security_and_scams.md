# ShopNest Security and Scam Safety

## Our official channels
ShopNest sells only on the ShopNest website and the ShopNest app.
Our support email is support@shopnest.example. We do not use any other email address or social media account for payments.
If you paid on another website or to a personal account, the order is not with ShopNest and we cannot track that payment.

## We never ask for your OTP or password
ShopNest staff never ask for your password, card PIN, CVV or one time code, by phone, email, SMS or chat.
Never share these with anyone who claims to be from ShopNest.

## Fake calls, messages and links
We do not send payment links by WhatsApp or SMS, except the tracking link for an order you placed.
If you get a message offering a prize, a refund by link or an urgent payment, do not click it.
Forward it to support@shopnest.example and we will look into it.

## Report a fraudulent charge
If you see a charge you did not make, contact support with your order ID or the transaction date and amount.
Payment problems are handled first, and a senior support specialist contacts you by email.
You can also report the charge to your bank or card provider.

## How we protect your data
Your card details are handled by our payment partner and are not stored by ShopNest.
We use your phone number and address only to deliver your order and to contact you about it.
