# ShopNest Order Management

## Cancel an order
An order can be cancelled for free before it is dispatched, from My Orders > Cancel Order.
The refund for a cancelled prepaid order goes to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the parcel at delivery, or return it under the return policy.

## Change an order
Items, size and colour cannot be changed after the order is placed. Cancel the order before dispatch and place a new one.
The delivery address can be changed only before dispatch, from My Orders > Edit Address.

## Order confirmation not received
After you pay, a confirmation email and SMS are sent within 15 minutes. Check My Orders to see whether the order was placed.
If the money was deducted but the order does not appear in My Orders, the amount is refunded automatically within 5 to 7 business days.

## Order shows delivered but not received
Check with neighbours and the building reception, because the courier may have left the parcel with them.
If you still cannot find it, report it within 48 hours of the delivered status from My Orders > Report a Problem.
We investigate with the courier and send a replacement or a refund once the investigation is complete.

## Missing item in the parcel
If an item is missing from a parcel with several items, report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report a Problem with a photo of the package and the invoice.

## Cancel part of an order
Items from one order that ship together cannot be cancelled one by one after dispatch.
Before dispatch, you can cancel the whole order and place a new order with the items you still want.

## Refused or undelivered parcels
If the courier cannot deliver after 3 attempts, the parcel returns to our warehouse.
We refund the order after the parcel is received, minus the shipping charge if any.
