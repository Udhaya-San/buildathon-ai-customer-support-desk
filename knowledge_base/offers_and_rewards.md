# ShopNest Offers and Rewards

## Coupons and discount codes
Discount codes are entered at checkout in the Apply Coupon box.
Each code has an expiry date and a minimum order value, both shown on the offer page.
A code that has expired or is below the minimum order value cannot be applied.
Only one coupon can be used per order, and coupons cannot be combined with other offers.

## ShopNest Wallet cashback
Selected orders earn cashback in ShopNest Wallet, as shown on the product page.
Cashback is credited within 7 days after the order is delivered.
Cashback is not credited on orders that are returned or cancelled.
Wallet balance can be used on any future order and is valid for 12 months from the day it is credited.

## First order offer
New customers get Rs 100 off on their first order of Rs 799 or more.
The offer is applied automatically at checkout and cannot be used together with a coupon code.

## Festival sale prices
During a sale, the price you pay is the price shown at the time you place the order.
If a price drops after you order, we do not adjust the price of an order that is already placed.
Sale items follow the normal return policy.

## Coupon did not work
If a valid coupon is not accepted at checkout, check the expiry date, the minimum order value and whether another offer is already applied.
If it still does not work, contact support with a screenshot of the coupon and the checkout page.
