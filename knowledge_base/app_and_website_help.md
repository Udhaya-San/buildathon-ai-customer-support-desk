# ShopNest App and Website Help

## The app will not open or keeps closing
Update the ShopNest app to the latest version from your phone's app store.
Restart your phone and open the app again. If it still closes, uninstall and reinstall the app. Your orders and wallet balance are saved in your account and are not lost.

## The website or checkout page is not loading
Check your internet connection and refresh the page.
Try another browser, or clear the cache of your current browser.
If checkout fails after you click Pay, check My Orders before you try again, so that you do not pay twice.

## Notifications are not coming
Turn on notifications for the ShopNest app in your phone settings.
Order updates are also sent by email and SMS, so check your spam folder for emails.

## Update your delivery details in the app
Save or change addresses from My Account > Addresses.
An address saved here is used for new orders only. An order already placed keeps its old address, as explained in the order management policy.

## Report a problem with the app
Send the app version, your phone model and a screenshot of the problem to support@shopnest.example.
