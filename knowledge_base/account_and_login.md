# ShopNest Account and Login Help

## Reset a forgotten password
1. Open the ShopNest login page and tap Forgot Password.
2. Enter the email address or mobile number registered with your account.
3. We send a reset link by email, or a 6 digit code by SMS. The link or code is valid for 30 minutes.
4. Open the link, then choose a new password with at least 8 characters, including one number and one symbol.
5. Log in with the new password.

## Reset email not received
Check your spam or promotions folder and wait up to 10 minutes.
Make sure you entered the same email address you used to register.
If you still do not receive it after 10 minutes, contact support from the Help page with your registered email address.

## Account locked
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes for your security.
After 30 minutes you can try again, or you can reset your password to unlock it immediately.

## Change your email or mobile number
Go to Settings > Profile and tap Edit next to the email or mobile number.
We send a one time code to the new email or number to confirm the change.

## Delete your account
Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete Account.
Deletion is completed within 7 days. Pending orders must be delivered or cancelled first.
