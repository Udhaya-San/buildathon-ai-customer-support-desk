# ShopNest Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days from the dispatch date.
Express delivery takes 2 to 3 business days and costs Rs 149 extra.
Same-day delivery is available in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi for orders placed before 11 AM.
Orders are processed and dispatched within 24 hours of payment confirmation.
Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free for orders of Rs 999 or more.
Orders below Rs 999 have a flat shipping charge of Rs 59.

## Tracking your order
Once your order is dispatched we email you a tracking link and an SMS with the courier name.
You can also track the order any time under My Orders > Track Order on the ShopNest website or app.
Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear after the courier picks up the parcel.

## Delayed orders
If your order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, please wait 1 business day and check the tracking page.
If it is still not delivered after that, contact support with your order ID. We raise a trace request with the courier and reply within 48 hours.
If your order is delayed by more than 7 business days past the estimated delivery date, you can choose to cancel it and receive a full refund or ask us to ship a replacement.

## Lost parcels
If the tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, the parcel is treated as lost.
For a lost parcel we send a replacement at no cost or refund the full amount, whichever you prefer.

## Wrong address
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is dispatched, from My Orders > Edit Address.
Once the order is dispatched the address cannot be changed.
